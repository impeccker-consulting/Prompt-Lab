# Prompt Lab: Gen AI for Compensation & Benefits Analytics
*Impeccker Consulting*

An interactive training exercise that teaches you to write effective prompts
for Gen AI tools. Improve a starter prompt, earn points and badges, then use
your best prompt to build your own analytics deliverable.

**Open the lab:** https://impeccker-consulting.github.io/Prompt-Lab/

## How it works
1. Choose or write a scenario and list your fields (names and meanings only).
2. Improve the prompt. The coach scores it, flags filler words and shows
   token waste.
3. Copy your best prompt into your Gen AI tool (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and others).

## Privacy
The page runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent or stored.
No real data is needed or should be entered. Share field names and meanings
only, never employee records.
