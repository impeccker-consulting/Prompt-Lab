    Safe to share with the AI
    • Field names, data types, allowed values
    • Business context and the questions to answer
    • Error messages (text only)
    Never share with the AI
    • Employee-level rows or IDs
    • Screenshots showing real records
    • Salary tables or pasted extracts

    Why local analysis is as good as, or better than, asking a chatbot to analyse data

    • Exact: R calculates on every row. Chatbots can miscalculate on large tables.
    • Auditable: every number traces back to code you can read, question and rerun.
    • Repeatable: run the same method every review cycle and compare results over time.
    • Governed: confidential compensation data never leaves your control.

    From prompt to dashboard

    1. Keep your CSV ready on your computer. You will load it into the app in step 7. It is not attached to the AI.
    2. Paste your 100% prompt into your AI tool. Attach nothing.
    3. Review the assumptions the AI lists, then reply "Go ahead".
    4. Save the code as app.R in a new folder.
    5. Install packages once, in the RStudio console: 
      install.packages(c("shiny","dplyr","plotly","DT"))
    6. Click "Run App" in RStudio.
    7. Load your CSV using the upload control in the app (ask for one in your prompt). Check that the fields were read as expected.
    8. Verify: compare the headline number with a quick calculation, test the filters on a small segment, and check that every chart has an explanation.

    If something breaks, iterate

    Do not start over. Paste the exact error text (never data rows) and ask for a fix. One change per message works best.

    • "I get this error: [paste error]. Explain the cause, then give me the corrected code."
    • "The filters are not updating the charts. Find out why and fix it."
    • "One chart is hard to read because a few large values dominate the scale. Fix the scaling."
    • "Add a before-and-after chart for the what-if scenario."
    • "Explain the results in plain English for a senior leader with no statistics background."
